Best Vintage Mirrors to Search For

How to search for vintage mirrors by shape, frame material, scale, condition, and room placement.

Start here

A vintage mirror can make a room feel larger, brighter, and more finished when the scale and frame suit the space.

Art Deco mirrors

Search art deco mirror when you want geometry, curves, brass, or a more polished room mood.

Carved wood mirrors

Wood frames are easier to blend into relaxed rooms and can warm up white walls.

Small accent mirrors

Small mirrors work in bathrooms, entry corners, and gallery walls when a large mirror would dominate.

Buyer checklist

Look for

  • Overall dimensions
  • Mirror glass condition
  • Frame depth
  • Mounting hardware

Be careful with

  • Silvering damage not shown
  • No weight listed
  • Frame cracks
  • Unclear hanging method
