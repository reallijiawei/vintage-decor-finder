How to Choose Vintage Lighting

A guide to vintage table lamps, brass lamps, sconces, shades, scale, wiring, and warm room light.

Guide focus

Check scale first

Measure the table, nightstand, or wall before falling for a lamp. A beautiful base can still be too tall, too wide, or visually heavy.

Inspect shade and wiring

Look for shade stains, dents, missing hardware, socket notes, and rewiring details. Budget for a new shade if the base is strong.

Use warm pools of light

Vintage rooms look better with layered table lamps and sconces than one bright overhead light.

Buyer checklist

Look for

  • Clear height and width
  • Shade condition shown
  • Wiring status described
  • Bulb type and socket visible

Be careful with

  • No scale reference
  • Unclear wiring
  • Burn marks near socket
  • Shade damage not photographed
