Room guide

Vintage Bathroom Decor

Upgrade the room with a mirror, tray, towel palette, glass object, and small print.

Build the room in the right order

For a vintage bathroom, start with pieces that change the mood without forcing a full redesign. The safest first buys are practical, visible, and easy to move if the layout changes.

Buy first

  • mirror
  • small tray
  • towel color
  • glass object
  • tiny wall print

Skip for now

Do not overcrowd the counter. Moisture and clutter make vintage pieces look accidental.

Starter checklist

  • Pick one vintage anchor instead of filling the whole room at once.
  • Repeat one metal finish and one wood tone.
  • Use lighting to make the room feel collected, not staged.
  • Keep one modern element so the room does not feel dated.
