Style guide

Art Deco Inspired Decor

Mirrors, fluting, marble trays, brass details, and evening-room polish.

How this style works

Art Deco Inspired Decor works best when the room has one clear anchor and a few repeated materials. Start with fluted glass, marble trays, arched mirrors, then add smaller accents only after the room direction is clear.

Best materials

  • fluted glass
  • marble trays
  • arched mirrors
  • brass
  • deep burgundy

Common mistake

Balance shine with velvet, matte black, or wood so the room still feels livable.

What to shop first

  • One vintage lighting piece with visible character.
  • One framed artwork or mirror to set the mood.
  • Two small accessories that repeat the room palette.
  • One texture piece: velvet, linen, wood, glass, brass, or ceramic.
