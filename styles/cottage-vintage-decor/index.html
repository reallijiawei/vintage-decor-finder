Style guide

Cottage Vintage Decor

Stoneware, botanical prints, linen, baskets, soft wood, and garden-inspired details.

How this style works

Cottage Vintage Decor works best when the room has one clear anchor and a few repeated materials. Start with stoneware, linen, basketry, then add smaller accents only after the room direction is clear.

Best materials

  • stoneware
  • linen
  • basketry
  • botanical prints
  • soft wood

Common mistake

Leave open shelf space. Cottage vintage looks better collected than packed.

What to shop first

  • One vintage lighting piece with visible character.
  • One framed artwork or mirror to set the mood.
  • Two small accessories that repeat the room palette.
  • One texture piece: velvet, linen, wood, glass, brass, or ceramic.
