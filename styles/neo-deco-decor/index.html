Style guide

Neo Deco Decor

Art Deco geometry, brass, black accents, glossy shapes, and apartment-friendly drama.

How this style works

Neo Deco Decor works best when the room has one clear anchor and a few repeated materials. Start with black lacquer, brass, smoked glass, then add smaller accents only after the room direction is clear.

Best materials

  • black lacquer
  • brass
  • smoked glass
  • velvet
  • geometric rugs

Common mistake

Use brass as punctuation, not the whole sentence. Black, cream, and green keep it grounded.

What to shop first

  • One vintage lighting piece with visible character.
  • One framed artwork or mirror to set the mood.
  • Two small accessories that repeat the room palette.
  • One texture piece: velvet, linen, wood, glass, brass, or ceramic.
