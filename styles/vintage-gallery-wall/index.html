Style guide

Vintage Gallery Wall

Landscape prints, portraits, botanical art, carved frames, and collected wall layouts.

How this style works

Vintage Gallery Wall works best when the room has one clear anchor and a few repeated materials. Start with carved frames, landscape prints, portraits, then add smaller accents only after the room direction is clear.

Best materials

  • carved frames
  • landscape prints
  • portraits
  • botanical art
  • aged paper

Common mistake

Do not hang every frame at a different visual weight. Repeat one finish or mat tone.

What to shop first

  • One vintage lighting piece with visible character.
  • One framed artwork or mirror to set the mood.
  • Two small accessories that repeat the room palette.
  • One texture piece: velvet, linen, wood, glass, brass, or ceramic.
